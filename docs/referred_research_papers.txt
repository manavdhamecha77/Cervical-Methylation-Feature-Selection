RESEARCH PAPERS AND DATASETS REFERRED TO IN OUR DISCUSSION
Prepared: 2026-10-13

This is a simple reference list of the papers and public datasets explicitly
discussed for this project. The datasets are listed separately from papers.
Background sensor papers are not validation studies of our cervical marker
panel or hardware.

RESEARCH PAPERS

1. Verlaat, W.; Snoek, B. C.; Heideman, D. A. M.; et al. (2018).
   Identification and Validation of a 3-Gene Methylation Classifier for
   HPV-Based Cervical Screening on Self-Samples.
   Clinical Cancer Research, 24(14), 3456-3464.
   DOI: 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-17-3615
   https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-17-3615
   Why it came up: The study built and independently validated a classifier
   using ASCL1, LHX8, and ST6GALNAC5 for CIN3 detection in hrHPV-positive
   lavage and brush self-samples. Its assay and cohorts differ from ours.

2. El-Zein, M.; Cheishvili, D.; Szyf, M.; Franco, E. L. (2024).
   Validation of Novel DNA Methylation Markers in Cervical Precancer and Cancer.
   International Journal of Cancer, 154(1), 104-113.
   DOI: 10.1002/ijc.34686
   https://doi.org/10.1002/ijc.34686
   Why it came up: The paper evaluates methylation markers including CA10,
   DPP10, FMN2, and HAS1 and uses public datasets including GSE99511 and
   GSE46306 as part of its analyses. It is relevant evidence, but it does not
   validate our exact selected panel or trained model.

3. Widiasti, I.; Novianti, P. W. (2026).
   Integrative Analyses of Individual Patient Genomic-Data to Discover Novel
   Biomarkers: Application to Cervical Pre-Cancer DNA-Methylation Datasets.
   Cancer Informatics, 25, 1.
   DOI: 10.1177/11769351261454663
   https://doi.org/10.1177/11769351261454663
   Why it came up: The paper combines evidence across four GEO methylation
   studies and reports candidate genes. It supports looking for external
   cohorts and cross-study evidence; it does not directly validate our chosen
   CpG panel or model.

4. Biagi, L.; Ramkissoon, C. M.; Facchinetti, A.; Leal, Y.; Vehi, J. (2017).
   Modeling the Error of the Medtronic Paradigm Veo Enlite Glucose Sensor.
   Sensors, 17(6), 1361.
   DOI: 10.3390/s17061361
   https://doi.org/10.3390/s17061361
   Why it came up: It models measurement noise, calibration error, and drift
   for a continuous glucose monitor. Its error distributions and parameter
   values do not apply to our sensor.

5. Sedlackova, E.; Bytesnikova, Z.; Birgusova, E.; Svec, P.; Ashrafi, A. M.;
   Estrela, P.; Richtera, L. (2020).
   Label-Free DNA Biosensor Using Modified Reduced Graphene Oxide Platform as
   a DNA Methylation Assay.
   Materials, 13(21), 4936.
   DOI: 10.3390/ma13214936
   https://doi.org/10.3390/ma13214936
   Why it came up: It reports calibration and repeatability experiments for a
   DNA methylation biosensor. Its analyte and assay differ from our proposed
   kit, so measured performance values should not be transferred.


PUBLIC DATASETS USED IN THIS PROJECT

A. GEO: GSE99511
   Title: Identification and validation of a novel 3-gene methylation
   classifier for HPV-based cervical screening on self-samples.
   GEO record: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE99511
   Project use: Main development cohort in our analysis. Our current
   Normal-versus-CIN3 subset contains 28 Normal and 36 CIN3 samples; cancer
   samples are excluded from that binary task.

B. GEO: GSE46306
   GEO record: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE46306
   Project use: Additional evaluation cohort. The project’s processed
   Normal-versus-CIN3 subset contains 20 Normal and 18 CIN3 samples; cancer
   samples are excluded from that binary task. Its available labels and HPV
   status differ from GSE99511, so performance comparisons require caution.
