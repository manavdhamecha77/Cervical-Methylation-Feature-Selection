01 Project Overview & Clinical Triage Mandate

Clinical Reflex Triage

Primary screening for cervical cancer using high-risk human papillomavirus (hrHPV) DNA testing has dramatically improved sensitivity compared to traditional Pap cytology. However, hrHPV testing suffers from low clinical specificity: over 80% of hrHPV infections are transient or produce low-grade, self-clearing lesions (≤ CIN1). Directly referring all hrHPV-positive women to colposcopy and biopsy leads to massive over-referral, overtreatment, patient anxiety, cervical scarring, and increased risk of preterm delivery in future pregnancies.

The Reflex Triage Objective: Develop an affordable, objective host-cell DNA methylation reflex test performed directly on residual cervical scrape/liquid-based cytology specimens. The test must triage hrHPV-positive women by identifying those with true transforming high-grade precancer (CIN3+ / cervical intraepithelial neoplasia grade 3 and occult invasive cancer) requiring colposcopy, while safely sparing women with transient infections from invasive procedures.

Intended Clinical Target Population

  • Target Population: Women aged 25–65 testing positive for high-risk HPV (hrHPV) in primary cervical screening.
  • Target Lesion (Positives): Histologically confirmed CIN3+ (CIN3, adenocarcinoma in situ [AIS], and invasive cervical cancer).
  • Clinical Controls (Negatives): hrHPV-positive women with Normal cytology/histology, transient infection, or low-grade CIN1.
  • Clinical Safety Goal: Sensitivity ≥ 85–90% to avoid missing high-grade precancerous lesions that could progress.

Summary Cohort Dimensions

  • Development Cohort: 209 hrHPV+ clinical specimens (GSE99511 [n=64] + GSE287994 [n=145]).
  • Untouched External Cohort 1: GSE46306 (n=38; 20 Normal vs. 18 CIN3; Illumina 450K cervical scrapes).
  • Untouched External Cohort 2: GSE143752 (n=146; 104 Normal vs. 42 CIN3; Illumina EPIC 850K cervical swabs).
  • Common Feature Space: 310,014 CpGs harmonized across Illumina 450K and EPIC array platforms.

Strict Preprocessing & Anti-Leakage Protocol

  • Platform Harmonization: Direct inner-join intersection across 450K and EPIC array probe coordinates yielded 310,014 identical CpG loci. Probes with cross-reactive hybridization or overlapping single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) were excluded.
  • Beta Matrix Extraction: All methylation values represent bounded ratio values β = M / (M + U + 100), bounded in [0, 1].
  • Strict Training-Fold-Only ComBat: To prevent subtle laboratory/chip batch bias from artificially inflating performance, Empirical Bayes batch adjustment (ComBat) was fitted strictly on training folds only. Validation and external test folds were adjusted solely using frozen parameters learned from the training split.
  • Missing Value Imputation: Missing probe signals were imputed using training-fold medians with zero look-ahead into test sets.

Detailed Domain Shift & Cross-Cohort Performance Analysis

Why did performance degrade on external cohorts?

  • Sample Collection Modality Differences: GSE143752 utilized dry cervical swabs, whereas GSE99511 and GSE287994 utilized liquid-based cervical brush cytology (ThinPrep). Swab specimens pick up predominantly superficial exfoliated epithelial cells and vaginal flora with variable proportion of basal cells, altering background methylation fractions.
  • Array Platform Hybridization Shift: While 310,014 probes are physically shared between 450K and EPIC, differences in fluorophore chemistry, bead pool densities, and scanner optics introduce residual shifts that cross-platform ComBat can only partially normalize when testing on isolated external cohorts.
  • Clinical Lesson: A single frozen threshold learned on liquid-based cytology cannot be naively transferred to swabs without assay-specific recalibration. This demonstrates why claims of universal AUC > 0.80 across diverse clinic cohorts without wet-lab assay standardization are premature.

Engineering Requirements for Point-of-Care Hardware:
  • Incubation Time: At τ = 5.0 min, at least 15 minutes of hybridization (θ ≥ 0.75) is required before read-out. Shorter incubation (≤ 5 min) causes dramatic loss in discrimination due to insufficient probe-target hybridization.
  • Maximum Tolerable Noise: The 10-CpG biomarker panel maintains acceptable discrimination provided biosensor instrumental noise σ ≤ 0.05. When noise exceeds σ = 0.10, baseline variation completely masks dysplastic hypomethylation/hypermethylation signals.

08 Research Conclusions, Clinical Limitations & Roadmap

Scientific Synthesis

Key Research Achievements

  • Multi-Cohort Expansion: Expanded beyond the 64-sample bottleneck by integrating GSE287994, GSE46306, and GSE143752, curating 393 clinical specimens harmonized to 310,014 common CpGs.
  • Published Baseline Reproduction: Rigorously reproduced the Verlaat et al. 3-gene classifier (ASCL1, LHX8, ST6GALNAC5), demonstrating an empirical array AUC of 0.6835–0.7004.
  • Stability-Selected Consensus Panels: Executed 100-bootstrap RFE stability selection, yielding compact panels (5, 10, 20, 50 CpGs) with quantified stability metrics (Kuncheva Index = 0.2153).
  • Illumina Manifest Verification: Resolved contradictory gene annotations from provisional logs, mapping all 10 probes to their true Illumina GPL13534 genomic loci.
  • Zero-Leakage Benchmark: Demonstrated strict nested cross-validation with in-fold ComBat batch adjustment, establishing an honest development AUC benchmark of 0.61–0.65.

Objective Status of the Target AUC ≥ 0.80

The AUC 0.80 Target Was NOT Met on Independent External Data:

While development nested-CV reached AUC 0.6450 (10-CpG XGBoost) and external GSE46306 reached AUC 0.6833 (50-CpG XGBoost), untouched external validation on GSE143752 yielded AUC 0.5098–0.5689.

Target AUC ≥ 0.80 remains an active research target under investigation, not a proven claim. Earlier claims of AUC > 0.80 in single-cohort splits were inflated by data leakage or resubstitution optimism.

Clinical Triage Assessment

In a clinical cervical screening workflow, the reflex triage test operates as a safety gate. Our provisional candidate model achieves high sensitivity (~88–90%), ensuring that very few high-grade transforming precancers (CIN3+) are missed (low false-negative rate). However, the model exhibits moderate-to-low specificity (~26–35%), meaning that a significant portion of hrHPV-positive women without high-grade precancer would still be referred to colposcopy.

Concrete Next Steps

  1. Formal EGA Data Access Application: Apply to the Data Access Committee (DAC) for access to the 1,254-sample WID-CIN cohort (EGAS00001005078) to increase development sample size by 6-fold.
  2. Dual Host-Viral Methylation Triage: Integrate host CpG methylation with HPV16/18 viral promoter methylation (L1/L2 and URR) or viral load genotyping to provide the necessary boost in clinical specificity.
  3. Wet-Lab Biosensor Prototyping: Synthesize synthetic DNA targets for the 10 verified CpG loci and test electrochemical hybridization kinetics on a physical biosensor electrode under the simulated noise tolerances.
