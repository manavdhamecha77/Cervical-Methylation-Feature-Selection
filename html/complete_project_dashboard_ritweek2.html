2. Multi-Cohort Integration & Harmonization

To eliminate sample-size bottlenecks, we integrated high-priority EPIC 850K cohorts:

  • GSE287994 (Bowden et al. 2025): 241 women (145 hrHPV-positive: 29 Benign, 116 CIN3/Cancer). Includes published PAX1 (cg16767801) + NREP-AS1 (cg23642047) biomarkers.
  • GSE143752 (Cheishvili et al. 2020): 186 samples with CIN progression and HPV genotyping. Used as external cross-cohort validation.
  • WID-CIN Study (PMC9580141, 1,254 samples): Access audit confirmed data is held in EGA (`EGAS00001005078`) under controlled access requiring DAC authorization.
