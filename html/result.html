Small CpG panel benchmark

Generated from stratified 5-fold cross-validation. The target is Normal versus CIN3 detection. Each approach selects original CpG probes; no PCA or latent feature reduction is used. Feature selection and scaling are fit inside each training fold.

Interpretation: the retained-feature percentage is k divided by the full cohort probe count. This is not a direct measurement of biological information loss. The observed change in cross-validation metrics across panel sizes is the practical task-specific measure of how much predictive signal the panel retained. Scores are exploratory and do not establish clinical reliability.

Methods

Within each training fold: mean imputation, variance filtering to at most 1,000 probes, selector fit, standardization, and classifier fit. Test-fold samples are held out from every fitting step. All seven selectors are evaluated at 5, 10, 20, and 50 CpGs with logistic regression and linear SVM. Stratified CV uses one 5-fold pass per dataset (5 outer-fold evaluations); classifiers use fixed C=1, without per-fold hyperparameter tuning. For tractability, stability selection uses 10 stratified subsamples, RF importance uses 100 trees, and SVM-RFE removes 50% per step. The variance prefilter is a screening step and may discard useful low-variance CpGs. Metrics are mean ± standard deviation across the five outer folds; uncertainty is high with only one CV repeat.

Datasets

  • GSE99511: 64 samples (Normal=28, CIN3=36), 405,336 CpGs before the fold-level variance prefilter.
  • GSE46306: 38 samples (Normal=20, CIN3=18), 451,345 CpGs before the fold-level variance prefilter.

GSE46306 values were inverted from GEO's arcsine-square-root beta transform using sin(x)². Its Normal controls are HPV-negative and CIN3 cases HPV-positive, so its within-cohort scores are confounded by HPV status and are exploratory. GSE99511 contains 28 Normal and 36 CIN3 samples for the primary comparison.

Conclusion

These runs show that small CpG panels can be evaluated, but do not establish a final or clinically reliable classifier. Use balanced accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, AUC, precision, and marker stability together; raw accuracy alone can hide missed CIN3 or excess false positives. GSE46306's higher within-cohort scores are confounded because HPV-negative controls are compared with HPV-positive CIN3 cases. The separate cross-cohort check also has low specificity. A 50-marker panel retains only about 0.012% of the GSE99511 CpG input and 0.011% of the GSE46306 CpG input, but this ratio is not a biological information-loss estimate. The observed metric changes across k are the empirical estimate of task-specific predictive signal retained. Validate a locked panel and threshold on a larger independent cohort with HPV status balanced across diagnosis before choosing a kit.
