# Cervical Cancer Methylation Feature Selection Benchmark: Branch ritweek2

## 1. Executive Summary & Clinical Context
This benchmark addresses the triage problem for cervical cancer screening: identifying high-risk cervical precancer (CIN3+) specifically among hrHPV-positive women. By expanding beyond the original 64-sample cohort to incorporate GSE287994 (Bowden et al. 2025, EPIC 850K) and GSE143752 (Cheishvili et al. 2020), the hrHPV-positive development cohort was expanded to **209 real clinical samples** across 450K and EPIC platforms.
